Your project inquiry

How we handle your inquiry.

What you send

The contact form asks for your name, email and requested service, with optional business, phone, budget and project details. A selected demo or offer and the page address may accompany your request.

What the form is for

We use your inquiry to understand the project and reply. The site is hosted on Cloudflare; form requests are forwarded through our configured Google Apps Script project system. Email and Instagram links open their respective services and follow those services’ policies.

Keep sensitive records out of the form

Please do not submit passwords, payment information, patient records or private client files. If a project needs access to a business tool, access arrangements will be discussed separately.

Questions or corrections

To ask about an inquiry, request a correction or ask us to delete submitted details, contact iluminity.studio@gmail.com.
